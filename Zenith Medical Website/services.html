Core Service

Family Medicine

Our family physicians provide continuous, comprehensive care for patients of all ages — from newborns to seniors. We focus on building long-term relationships so we can understand your health history and support you through every stage of life.

This includes diagnosis and treatment of acute and ongoing conditions, coordination with specialists, and general health guidance.

  • Diagnosis and treatment of illness and injury
  • Ongoing health monitoring
  • Coordination of specialist referrals
  • Care for patients of all ages
Core Service

Preventive Care

Preventing illness before it starts is at the heart of family medicine. We offer periodic health checkups, age- and risk-appropriate screenings, and immunizations to help you stay ahead of potential health concerns.

  • Annual and periodic health exams
  • Cancer and chronic disease screening
  • Vaccinations, including seasonal flu clinics
  • Lifestyle and risk-factor counselling
Core Service

Chronic Disease Management

Living with a chronic condition requires consistent, coordinated support. We work with patients managing diabetes, hypertension, asthma, and other ongoing conditions to develop personalized care plans and monitor progress over time.

  • Individualized chronic condition care plans
  • Regular monitoring and lab follow-up
  • Medication management
  • Education and self-management support
Core Service

Women's Health

We provide comprehensive women's health services, including reproductive health counselling, preventive screening, and referrals for prenatal and specialist care as needed.

  • Preventive screening and wellness exams
  • Reproductive and sexual health counselling
  • Prenatal care referrals
  • Menopause and hormonal health support
Core Service

Children's Health

From well-baby checkups to childhood immunizations and growth monitoring, our team supports the healthy development of your children in a warm, family-friendly environment.

  • Well-child and well-baby visits
  • Childhood immunization schedules
  • Growth and development monitoring
  • Support for common childhood illnesses
Core Service

Mental Health

Mental health is an essential part of overall wellbeing. Our physicians offer compassionate assessment, ongoing support, and referrals to mental health specialists and community resources when needed.

  • Mental health assessments
  • Ongoing support and follow-up
  • Referrals to counselling and psychiatric services
  • Confidential, judgment-free conversations
Core Service

Seniors' Health

We support healthy, independent aging through comprehensive geriatric assessments, medication reviews, and coordinated care for age-related and chronic conditions.

  • Comprehensive geriatric assessments
  • Medication reviews
  • Fall-risk and mobility support
  • Coordination with home care and specialists
Core Service

Minor Procedures

Our clinic offers a range of in-office minor procedures, performed by our physicians in a comfortable, clinical setting.

  • Minor skin lesion removal
  • Wound care and suturing
  • Joint and soft-tissue injections
  • Ear irrigation and related minor procedures

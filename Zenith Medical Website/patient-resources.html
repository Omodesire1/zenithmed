Referrals

If your physician has referred you to a specialist, the referral will be sent directly to the specialist's office. Wait times vary by specialty and are set by the receiving office, not by our clinic.

Questions about the status of a referral? Contact our front desk at [PLACEHOLDER PHONE NUMBER].

Test Results

Your physician or a member of our clinical team will contact you regarding test results that require follow-up. If you have not heard back within [PLACEHOLDER: timeframe], please call the clinic.

Medical Records

To request a copy of your medical records or have them transferred to another provider, complete a Medical Records Release Form at the front desk. A processing fee may apply, in accordance with Ontario regulations.

See our Privacy Policy for more on how your health information is managed under Ontario's PHIPA.

Uninsured Services

Some services are not covered by OHIP and may involve a fee, such as certain forms, travel medicine consultations, and some medical certificates. A full fee schedule is available at the front desk.

After-Hours Care

For non-emergency care needs outside of our clinic hours, consider visiting a local walk-in clinic or contacting Health811 (dial 8-1-1) for 24/7 health advice from a registered nurse in Ontario.

Emergency

For medical emergencies, always call 911 or go to your nearest emergency department.

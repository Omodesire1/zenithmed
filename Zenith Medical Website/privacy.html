Privacy Policy

Last updated: [PLACEHOLDER DATE]

Zenith Medical Centre is committed to protecting the privacy and confidentiality of personal health information in accordance with Ontario's Personal Health Information Protection Act, 2004 (PHIPA), and the general privacy principles of the federal Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) where applicable.

Our Commitment

As a health information custodian under PHIPA, our clinic collects, uses, discloses, retains and disposes of personal health information responsibly and only as permitted or required by law, and generally with your consent.

What Information We Collect

We may collect personal health information such as your name, contact details, OHIP number, medical history, and clinical records, as necessary to provide you with care and to operate our clinic.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and coordinate your medical care
  • To communicate with you about appointments, test results and follow-up care
  • To bill OHIP or other applicable payors
  • To comply with legal and regulatory requirements

How We Protect Your Information

We maintain administrative, technical and physical safeguards to protect personal health information from unauthorized access, use or disclosure, consistent with PHIPA requirements.

Your Rights

Under PHIPA, you have the right to access and request correction of your personal health information, and to withdraw or limit consent to certain uses and disclosures, subject to legal and clinical limitations. To make a request, contact our Privacy Officer using the details below.

Website Contact Form

Our website contact form is intended for general, non-urgent inquiries only. Please do not submit personal health information or urgent medical concerns through the contact form. Information submitted through the contact form is not stored as part of your medical record.

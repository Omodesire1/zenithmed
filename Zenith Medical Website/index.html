About Zenith Medical Centre

Family medicine, practised with continuity and care.

We believe good primary care is built on relationships. Our physicians and clinical team work together to support your health across every stage of life — from infancy through to your senior years — with an emphasis on prevention, coordination and clear communication.

As an Ottawa-based family clinic, we're proud to serve the local community with accessible, evidence-based care close to home.

Looking for a Family Doctor?

Here's how registration works at Zenith Medical Centre, and our current patient registration status.

Register Now
Eligibility

Who can register?

We welcome patients of all ages who live in or around Ottawa, Ontario and hold a valid Ontario Health Insurance Plan (OHIP) card or equivalent provincial coverage.

What You'll Need

Documents to bring

  • Valid government-issued photo ID
  • OHIP card or valid provincial health card
  • List of current medications (if applicable)
  • Contact information for your previous family doctor, if any
How It Works

Registration steps

1. Submit your information

Complete our secure online registration form, or visit the clinic in person to fill out a paper registration form.

2. Confirmation

Our team will review your submission and contact you to confirm your registration or wait-list status.

3. Book your first visit

Once registered, you'll be matched with a family physician and can book your first appointment.

Secure registration portal link to be provided by clinic administration. Do not submit personal health information through the general contact form.
